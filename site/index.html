Beta — 0.1.0

Voice notes, transcribed on your device.

Record a meeting, a lecture, or a thought. Read the words as you speak them. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to.

Download for Mac Read the guide

It stays on your Mac

Transcription runs on Apple's on-device speech engine. Summaries use Apple Intelligence when you have it, and a built-in summarizer when you don't. No account, no server, no telemetry.

Your files, in your folder

Recordings are ordinary .m4a audio with plain-text transcripts beside them, in a folder you pick. There is no library to escape from and nothing to export — the files are already yours.

Both sides of a call

On the Mac, Transcripts captures your microphone and the other participants, notices when a call starts, and can tell the voices apart afterwards.

It follows you off the desk

Record on iPhone or iPad and it appears on the Mac through the folder you already share. Set up with iCloud on both and there is nothing to configure.

This is a beta. It has been used daily for months in an earlier form, but under this name it is new: expect rough edges, and please report anything that misbehaves to support@hatcher.ltd. Recording laws vary by country and state, and some require the consent of everyone in the conversation — Transcripts gives you a recorder, and the rest is yours.
