# Privacy Policy

**Transcripts for iPhone and iPad**
Last updated: 15 August 2026

## The short version

Transcripts collects nothing. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and
no advertising. It contains no networking code of any kind — not disabled, not
optional: absent. Your recordings, transcripts, and settings stay on your
device and in the folder you choose.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Audio recordings** are written on your device as ordinary `.m4a` files.
- **Transcripts** are stored alongside them as plain text in a small `.json`
  file per recording.
- **Settings** (your chosen folder, app preferences) are stored in the app's
  own preferences on your device.

When you choose a destination folder, recordings are copied there. If that
folder is inside a cloud service you already use — iCloud Drive, OneDrive,
Dropbox — then that service syncs the files under **its** privacy policy and
your account with them, exactly as it would for any other file you put in that
folder. Transcripts is not party to that transfer; it writes a file to a
folder your device has given it access to.

## Speech recognition

Live transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Transcripts sets
Apple's `requiresOnDeviceRecognition` flag, and additionally refuses to
transcribe at all on a device that cannot recognize speech locally — in that
case it turns live text off and tells you why, rather than falling back to
server-based recognition. Your audio is never sent to Apple's speech servers,
or anyone else's.

On devices with Apple Intelligence, summaries are generated by Apple's
on-device foundation model. This also runs locally.

## Microphone

Transcripts uses the microphone only while you are recording, and only after
you grant permission through iOS. You can revoke it at any time in
**Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Microphone**. Recording continues in the
background so a capture survives the screen locking — this is the point of a
pocket recorder, and iOS shows a persistent indicator whenever it is happening.

## What is never collected

No personal information, no contacts, no location, no device identifiers, no
usage analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, and no
tracking of any kind. Transcripts does not use third-party SDKs — it has no
third-party dependencies at all.

## Children

Transcripts is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Your recordings are your responsibility

Recording laws vary by country and by state. Some jurisdictions require the
consent of every participant before a conversation may be recorded. Transcripts
gives you a recorder; deciding whether you may lawfully record a given
conversation, and obtaining any consent required, is yours.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
